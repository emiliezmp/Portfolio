Peak Digital — Brand Guide

A brand guide created to define and communicate Peak Digital's visual identity consistently.

PURPOSE

The purpose of the brand guide was to provide clear guidelines for employees and others working with the Peak Digital brand. It ensures that the visual identity is applied consistently across different platforms and materials.

RESULT

The final brand guide provides Peak Digital with a practical reference for maintaining a consistent and recognizable visual identity.
